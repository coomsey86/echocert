# Who Buys EchoCert?

EchoCert is for people and organisations that need a simple, defensible integrity record around important digital files.

The commercial priority is **not everyone**. Early sales should focus on buyers who already feel the cost of weak evidence, disputes, missing records or messy field documentation.

---

## Priority 1 — Inspection, construction and field services

### Their problem

Photos and videos are often captured on ordinary phones, passed through messaging apps, renamed, compressed or separated from the job record.

When a client, inspector or contractor challenges what happened, the team has to reconstruct the evidence afterwards.

### EchoCert value

- Capture or preserve the original file
- Generate an integrity receipt automatically
- Re-verify the file later
- Associate evidence with a job/case reference
- Produce professional reports/evidence packs on desktop
- Keep the core workflow usable offline/local-first

### Buyer message

**Capture the job evidence once. Prove later that the file you kept is unchanged.**

---

## Priority 2 — Legal support and dispute preparation

### Their problem

Digital files regularly become part of complaints, claims and disputes, but clients often arrive with screenshots, renamed files or evidence stored without a reliable integrity record.

### EchoCert value

EchoCert can help create a clear integrity record around files and produce structured receipts and reports for professional review.

It does not decide admissibility, truth, authorship or legal ownership.

### Buyer message

**Preserve the file, record its integrity, and make later verification straightforward.**

---

## Priority 3 — Compliance and internal audit

### Their problem

Teams need repeatable records showing what files or outputs were reviewed and whether those records later changed.

### EchoCert value

- Consistent receipts
- SHA-256 verification
- Case/project metadata
- Evidence packs and reports
- Local-first handling for sensitive material
- AI-assisted workflow records where relevant

### Buyer message

**A lightweight integrity layer for records that may later need to be reviewed or challenged.**

---

## Priority 4 — Property, condition and insurance-support records

### Their problem

Condition photos, videos and documents can become disputed after damage, repair, tenancy, inspection or insurance events.

### EchoCert value

EchoCert can preserve an integrity record around the original digital file and make later byte-for-byte verification easy.

### Buyer message

**Keep the original. Keep the receipt. Check it later.**

---

## Priority 5 — Investigators and specialist consultants

### Their problem

Small investigation and consultancy teams may need defensible file handling but do not need — or cannot justify — a heavyweight forensic lab platform.

### EchoCert value

A focused local-first workflow for preservation, SHA-256 receipts, verification, metadata and reporting.

---

## Secondary market — AI governance and AI-assisted work

EchoCert began with prompt/output receipt and drift workflows. That remains a valid use case for:

- AI governance teams
- Compliance teams
- Researchers
- AI consultants and agencies
- Businesses documenting AI-assisted work

But AI auditing is now **one market within EchoCert**, not the entire product identity.

---

## Who is NOT the first customer

Early development should not chase:

- Consumers who only want decorative timestamps on photos
- Large police/forensic laboratories requiring mature accredited suites
- Enterprise customers requiring complex cloud infrastructure before a pilot
- Buyers demanding features unrelated to integrity, evidence or verification
- Markets where the only differentiator would be price

These can distract from proving the core product.

---

## Early commercial test

The first question is not “How many features can EchoCert have?”

It is:

**Will a real inspection, legal, compliance or field-work user choose EchoCert to protect a real evidence workflow — and will at least some of them pay to keep using it?**

Until that is answered, development should favour reliability, simplicity and pilot feedback over expansion.

---

## Simple buyer message

**EchoCert helps you preserve important digital files, create a SHA-256 integrity receipt, and verify later whether the file is still exactly the same. Mobile captures the evidence; desktop verifies, organises and reports it.**
