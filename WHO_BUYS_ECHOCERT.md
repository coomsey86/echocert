# Who Buys EchoCert?

EchoCert is for people and organisations that need better evidence around AI-assisted work.

---

## 1. Compliance teams

They need records showing how AI was used, what was generated and whether records changed later.

EchoCert helps by creating simple receipts and reports that can support review and governance.

---

## 2. AI governance teams

They need visibility over prompts, outputs, changes and evidence trails.

EchoCert gives them a practical way to record AI-assisted work without relying only on platform logs.

---

## 3. Legal support and dispute preparation

When AI content becomes part of a dispute, the record matters.

EchoCert helps preserve prompt/output evidence and show whether a file or receipt has been changed.

---

## 4. Internal audit teams

Auditors need clean records.

EchoCert can support internal AI audits by creating consistent receipts, verification checks and readable reports.

---

## 5. AI consultants and agencies

Consultants who deliver AI-assisted work need a way to show customers what was produced and when.

EchoCert can support professional evidence packs and client-facing documentation.

---

## 6. Researchers

Researchers using AI tools need repeatability, traceability and comparison between versions.

EchoCert can help record prompts, outputs and changes across experiments.

---

## 7. Small businesses adopting AI

Small businesses may not have formal AI governance systems.

EchoCert gives them a lightweight way to create basic records before AI use becomes messy.

---

## Simple buyer message

**If AI is involved in a decision, report, client deliverable or dispute, EchoCert helps create a record of what happened.**
