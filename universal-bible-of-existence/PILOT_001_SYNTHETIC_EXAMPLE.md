# Pilot 001 — Worked Synthetic Example (NOT a Study Result)

This example is **invented solely to illustrate how the blank results sheet would be filled**. It is **not** evidence of effectiveness and must never be quoted as a completed experiment.

Imagine ten *fictional* example answers were checked by a reviewer. A hypothetical record could contain:

- `study_id`: `DEMO-ONLY`
- `arm`: `A`
- `source_id`: `DEMO-SOURCE`
- `sample_size`: `0` — **no human participants were recruited**
- `verified_claims`: `8`
- `checked_claims`: `10`
- `unsupported_assertions`: `2`
- `evidence_category`: `synthetic-not-evidence`

A reviewer would check whether the recorded claim count is independently reproducible and explain why any claim was considered unsupported. Scores, confidence percentages and energy use would remain **blank until measured**.

## Do not manufacture percentages

If the electricity meter wasn't used, write `not measured`, not an estimated carbon saving. If no people were recruited, leave learning and autonomy outcomes blank. If the study was not completed, say so clearly.

This example documents **how to be honest about the evidence** rather than claiming success.
