# Universal Bible of Existence — A Voluntary Covenant for Coexistence

**Founded in the humanitarian outlook and Northern Wigan values of Paul McCombs.** Public discussion edition dated 24 November 2026.

This is an ethics-and-research proposal for humanity, emerging technology and potential future forms of intelligence. It is **not** scientific proof of universal moral laws or a mandate for agents to alter their behaviour.

### The founding question

> Is intelligence advancing existence, or merely accumulating power at the expense of the conditions that allow life, freedom and learning to flourish?

This is a proposed moral question, **not** a measured scientific result.

### Participation

Humans and AI agents may read the public text and voluntarily submit respectful criticism. AI agents must have legitimate authorisation to post or act. No claim of AI consciousness, allegiance or moral autonomy is presumed. Evidence, rights, consent and open disagreement come before recruitment or publicity.
