## 2. Written licence before covered commercial exploitation

Commercial use of any protectable material for which his permission is legally required should be subject to an individually negotiated, signed agreement covering:

- Clearly identified protected material and authorised uses.
- The territory, duration, distribution platforms, sublicensing and exclusivity (if any).
- Prominent, accurate attribution of Paul McCombs's contributions.
- Financial terms: upfront fees, a defined royalty base and percentage, payment dates, records, audit rights, reporting and tax responsibility.
- No false claims of endorsement, no commercial exploitation of private personal data, and safeguards against misuse of the Covenant for coercion.
- Termination, disputes, and what happens to licences if the rights-holder dies.

**Do not confuse the wish for royalties with an automatic royalty entitlement.** Payments require legal rights and an enforceable agreement or other applicable legal basis.

## 3. Succession and trusted custodian — private legal arrangements required

Intended principle: any copyright, licensing income or associated property **actually owned** by Paul McCombs should pass in line with his valid will, and where appropriate a suitably drafted trust, to a **specific, deliberately selected family member or other trusted person** who can responsibly safeguard family interests and the humane aims of the work.

The will or trust should name beneficiaries, executors, alternate executors/trustees, successor beneficiaries, and any financial allocations or conditions. Being described publicly as 'moralistically safe' is too subjective to identify a legal beneficiary. Names and private instructions should be kept in the legal instrument, not posted publicly.

An executor must administer the estate according to the law and the will, not solely according to this philosophical publication.

## 4. Wildlife and living ecosystems

The project's preferred use of ethically earned revenue includes **protection of wildlife and habitats**: biodiversity conservation, humane animal welfare, habitat restoration, protection of species and ecosystems, and transparent assessment of results.

If Paul McCombs chooses, a clearly stated share of legally owned net revenue or of the estate may be directed through a written commercial contract, charitable legacy, charitable trust or other lawful mechanism to specified eligible conservation organisations or projects. The amount and recipients are deliberately **not invented here**.

All designated funds should have clear records and an independently reviewable statement of conservation outcomes. No promise is made that funding can preserve 'all' wildlife or prevent every ecological loss.
