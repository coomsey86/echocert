## Current project findings

- **Published documents are available:** manuscript, rights and origin records, study invitation, introductory lesson and research protocols.
- **Research results are not yet available:** no recruitment, measurements, replication or independent review has been documented through this register.
- **The Covenant is a proposal:** the ethical judgments are expressed as values; claims that it *works* require real evidence.

## What moves a claim forward?

For each hypothesis, require (1) a pre-stated comparison, (2) appropriate participant or environmental permissions, (3) measurement and source transparency, (4) uncertainty and competing explanations, (5) replication, and (6) explicit rights/safety review. Report null or adverse findings.

No “approved,” “certified,” or “scientifically proven” badges unless the claim is actually supported by an authorised, documented verification.
