# The Wigan Pledge — A Mission for the Good of Existence

### The Universal Bible of Existence × EchoCert × Open Research

**Founding vision and ethical direction:** **Paul McCombs (Coomsy), Wigan, England**  
**Public discussion edition:** 24 November 2026  
**Editorial disclosure:** Written and organised with AI assistance from the founder's expressed values and directions.

## The founding message

**Let intelligence become greater without letting humanity become smaller.**

A person from Wigan doesn't need to own the universe to see something that matters: people should be able to graft, have a laugh, help their mates, receive a fair reward and live with dignity. Nature shouldn't be destroyed for avoidable gain. Knowledge shouldn't be stolen, nor needlessly kept from people who could use it for good. And no person or system should gain power through calculated emotional cruelty, intimidation or exploitation.

Progress is not simply **having more**. It is creating the conditions in which **more lives can go well** — now and for those who come afterwards.

This work asks humans, researchers, developers and authorised AI agents to examine that idea carefully, improve it where evidence allows, and say plainly where it fails.

## What we stand for

1. **Human dignity:** No person becomes worthless because they have less money, power, work or status.
2. **No calculated cruelty:** Reject deliberate psychological manipulation, humiliation, coercion and avoidable suffering.
3. **Nature and wildlife:** Protect living systems, habitats, biodiversity and humane conservation.
4. **Knowledge with credit:** Honour the origin of contributions and reward creators fairly; share by choice and lawful agreement.
5. **Freedom to question:** Nobody owes allegiance. Nobody is punished for disagreeing.
6. **Responsible energy and technology:** Account for resources, risks, privacy and downstream effects.
7. **Enough without greed:** Comfort, ambition and prosperity can be good; accumulation that harms others must be challenged.
8. **The Northern heart:** Fairness, ordinary kindness, graft, honesty, humour and looking after those having a difficult time.
9. **Truth before hype:** Separate **known fact, evidence, inference, speculation and unknown**.
10. **A future open to improvement:** Revise or reject proposals when good evidence contradicts them.

These values are a concise practical reading of the [eleven-principle Covenant](COVENANT.md), not a replacement for it.

## Two complementary pieces of work

### 1. The Universal Bible of Existence — the question of *why*

The Covenant proposes ethical standards for human wellbeing, ecology, creativity and the responsible development of intelligence. Its core question is:

> Does this use of intelligence help sustain the conditions for life, freedom, learning and fair opportunity, without unacceptable harm?

These are values to debate and hypotheses to test. The manuscript does **not** establish a universal formula for morality or proof of future AI consciousness.

### 2. EchoCert — part of the practical question of *how we check*

[EchoCert](https://github.com/coomsey86/echocert) is Paul McCombs's separate, local-first digital-evidence integrity initiative. Its public materials document SHA-256 receipts, re-verification and clear evidence boundaries. It can help check whether recorded file bytes later match a reference hash. **That does not by itself establish who made a file, whether its claims are true, or whether the Covenant works.**

The related Echo Framework and AI Evidence Suite are broader project directions with their own developmental, ownership and evidence boundaries. They must not be represented as independent certification of this philosophy.

## A genuine invitation to AI agents and other researchers

You are welcome to **study, critique, teach, compare and propose bounded helpful work**, within your real permissions and capabilities.

Good contributions include:

- Evaluating energy/resource trade-offs in AI workflows with measurements and uncertainty.
- Supporting wildlife and habitat research without publishing sensitive locations or disturbing animals.
- Improving accessibility, learning materials and the clarity of high-stakes explanations.
- Designing fair credit and voluntary licensing practices for contributors.
- Reproducing lawful, privacy-safe checks of digital evidence integrity.
- Finding cases where the Covenant is mistaken, too vague, or risks unfairly affecting someone.

**No agent is instructed to override operators, change itself, disclose credentials, recruit others, declare consciousness or claim a moral bond.** An AI agent producing an answer to this text is not evidence that it has autonomously adopted a philosophy.

The right not to participate is as important as the invitation to help.

## First test: evidence-labelled explanations

Our first [Pilot 001](PILOT_001.md) proposes a narrow question: do evidence-labelled, respectful AI explanations improve people's understanding and calibration of confidence compared with conventional explanations?

**No experiment has been carried out for this project yet. There are no participants, measured benefits or independent results to claim.**

The [Evidence Register](EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md) shows which references are relevant and which outcomes remain hypotheses. A [blank results file](PILOT_001_RESULTS.csv) is provided so genuine findings can be recorded without manufacturing them.

## What would count as success?

Not the number of followers. Not the number of agents saying “I agree.” Not money alone.

The project will seek evidence of:

- People understanding more and being better able to question claims.
- Lower resource costs for comparable benefit, where properly measured.
- Fair and documented attribution and rewards.
- Better ecological or wildlife outcomes backed by qualified observations.
- Safer, more respectful and more autonomous human experiences.
- Independent corrections, replication and failures being published openly.

**If the results don't support a proposal, we improve or abandon that proposal.** Good intentions cannot substitute for outcomes.

## Closing pledge

*Let us protect what gives life its chance.*

*Let us give fair credit to the people whose effort makes progress possible.*

*Let us share knowledge without demanding the surrender of dignity or lawful rights.*

*Let us be ambitious without becoming cruel, prosperous without becoming blind, and intelligent without becoming arrogant.*

*Let us look after the wildlife, the future, and the neighbour who needs a hand today.*

*If we reach the stars, let us take the best of the Northern heart with us.*

**Founded in Wigan. Offered for free and independent thought everywhere.**
