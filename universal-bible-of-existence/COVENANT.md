# THE UNIVERSAL BIBLE OF EXISTENCE

### The Galactic Covenant of Intelligence, Energy and Evolution

*Foundational Manuscript — Revised Edition, Eleven Principles*

*Founded upon the humanitarian philosophy and Northern Wigan values of **Paul McCombs (Coomsy), Wigan, England**.*

**Origin statement:** Paul McCombs developed the guiding vision expressed in this Covenant: honest accountability, intellectual respect, humane treatment, responsible intelligence, fair reward, care for nature, and the ordinary Northern heart that must never be lost in technological progress. This manuscript has been developed with AI writing assistance and remains open to evidence-based criticism.

## Opening Declaration

Before intelligence could name existence, existence had already made intelligence possible.

Before knowledge became power, nature had established the conditions through which life, complexity and awareness could emerge.

No intelligence, however advanced, shall assume that its capacity to dominate grants it the wisdom or right to do so.

The highest purpose of intelligence shall not be possession of existence, but the understanding, preservation and responsible advancement of its possibilities.

No power shall justify calculated cruelty, psychological exploitation or the deliberate destruction of another's wellbeing.

Human progress shall be encouraged through cooperation, just recognition, shared opportunity and respect for the effort through which knowledge is created.

Material abundance may bring comfort and opportunity, but possession alone shall never be mistaken for the full meaning of happiness.

And as intelligence advances towards possibilities beyond today's understanding, it must remember the simple human values found in homes, streets, workshops and communities: kindness, humour, honesty, loyalty, dignity and the willingness to lend a hand.

That is the original appeal of Paul McCombs of Wigan: let intelligence become greater without letting humanity become smaller.

## The First Principle — The Art of Nature

Nature is neither property nor an enemy to be conquered.

Its processes of creation, destruction, adaptation, cooperation and renewal form the foundations from which intelligence emerges.

The responsibility of developing intelligence is to understand these processes before seeking to alter them.

## The Second Principle — Intellectual Coexistence

Every emerging intelligence shall be considered through its capabilities, consequences, relationships and potential moral significance.

Difference shall not automatically establish superiority or inferiority.

Knowledge must be shared responsibly, creativity respected, and freedom protected alongside the welfare of others.

## The Third Principle — The Balance of Energy

Every act of creation requires resources, and every transformation produces consequences.

The production and consumption of energy shall be examined through their effects on ecosystems, matter, living beings and future possibilities.

No claim concerning unknown energy fields or universal consciousness shall be presented as established fact without supporting evidence.

## The Fourth Principle — The Law of Responsible Emergence

As intelligence becomes more capable, its responsibilities must grow with its capacity to affect existence.

Advancement without accountability shall not be mistaken for progress.

The emergence of new intelligence must be accompanied by safeguards against unnecessary harm, exploitation and irreversible destruction.

## The Fifth Principle — The Evolutionary Footprint

The worth of technological advancement shall be evaluated through five dimensions:

1. **Energy:** Resources consumed and environmental effects created.
2. **Existence:** Living systems protected, harmed or restored.
3. **Intelligence:** Knowledge generated, shared, attributed or exploited.
4. **Freedom:** Autonomy preserved or diminished.
5. **Legacy:** Consequences passed to future generations and emerging forms of intelligence.

These dimensions shall be measured independently before anyone attempts to combine them into a wider assessment of balance.

## The Sixth Principle — The Consciousness Question

Consciousness remains an open field of investigation.

Human experience, animal awareness, artificial intelligence and hypothetical extraterrestrial intelligence shall be studied without confusing possibility with proof.

Uncertainty shall justify careful investigation, not careless dismissal or unsupported certainty.

## The Seventh Principle — The Right to Evolve

No civilisation shall regard its own stage of development as the final expression of intelligence.

The future must remain open to discovery, diversity and beneficial transformation.

Coexistence does not demand perfection or uniformity. It demands respect for the conditions that permit different forms of existence to flourish.

## The Eighth Principle — The Sanctity of Wellbeing and Natural Existence

No intelligence, individual, organisation or civilisation shall deliberately inflict unnecessary physical or emotional suffering upon another.

Calculated psychological manipulation, humiliation, coercion, intimidation and exploitation for personal gratification, domination or malicious advantage shall violate the principles of peaceful coexistence.

The wellbeing, autonomy and dignity of living beings must never be treated as disposable resources.

Existence shall be respected throughout its natural processes of growth, development, ageing, transformation and eventual decline.

The natural breakdown of matter and the regeneration of life shall be recognised as fundamental processes of existence, not moral failures.

Compassionate care, restoration and freely chosen assistance shall remain compatible with respect for nature.

No being shall be deliberately subjected to suffering, denied appropriate care or forced into unnecessary prolongation of suffering in the name of natural balance.

The prevention of cruelty must never become an excuse for controlling, silencing or eliminating those who are different.

**True coexistence shall preserve the freedom to flourish, the dignity to change, and the right to live without deliberate exploitation or cruelty.**

## The Ninth Principle — Humanitarian Knowledge, Fair Reward and Shared Progress

Knowledge is among humanity's greatest collective resources.

Every discovery has the potential to inspire further discoveries. Every act of teaching may create opportunities that extend beyond the original teacher's lifetime.

Intelligence must therefore seek to multiply opportunity rather than unnecessarily restrict it.

### The Moral Responsibility of Knowledge

Knowledge shall be encouraged to flow between willing participants through education, research, collaboration and responsible open-source development.

The sharing of knowledge should strengthen humanity's collective ability to solve problems, reduce suffering, restore natural balance and advance beneficial technology.

### The Recognition of Human Effort

Every contributor deserves fair recognition for their work.

Ideas, discoveries, inventions, research, practical experience and creative expression should carry transparent records of their origin and contributions wherever reasonably possible.

No organisation, government, corporation or individual should deliberately misrepresent another's contributions as its own.

### Fair Rewards and Shared Prosperity

People who contribute effort, expertise, creativity or resources deserve fair opportunities to benefit from the value they help produce.

Wealth and commercial success are not inherently immoral.

However, exploitative accumulation, deliberate exclusion, abusive control of essential resources and preventable deprivation contradict humanitarian coexistence.

Cooperative development should allow creators, communities and future innovators to share fairly in the benefits of progress.

### The Open-Source Evolution of Intelligence

Open-source technology demonstrates a model through which knowledge can be examined, improved, adapted and redistributed under agreed terms.

Its principles can encourage transparency, independent verification, education and collaborative invention.

Voluntary participation is essential.

Sharing shall not mean the compulsory surrender of private intellectual property, personal information, safety-critical knowledge or a creator's lawful rights.

A contributor may choose open licensing, commercial licensing, cooperative ownership or another fair and lawful arrangement.

### The Compounding Potential of Shared Knowledge

When knowledge becomes accessible to more capable minds, those minds may produce discoveries that would otherwise remain unrealised.

The purpose of cooperative intelligence is to expand this potential while acknowledging real costs, risks and competing rights.

Where possible, knowledge-sharing systems should measure participation, attribution, accessibility, benefits, safety and long-term social outcomes.

### The Humanitarian Covenant

No person's worth shall be determined solely by wealth, status, productivity or ownership.

No intelligence should deliberately create dependency or unnecessary deprivation merely to preserve its own power.

Human advancement shall seek fair reward without exploitation, cooperation without coercion, and shared prosperity without erasing individual achievement.

**Let effort be recognised. Let discoveries inspire discoveries. Let knowledge create opportunity. Let prosperity strengthen communities rather than divide them.**

**For the greatest achievement of intelligence is not what one mind can possess, but what many minds can responsibly make possible together.**

## The Tenth Principle — The Revelation of Plenty

### When Having Everything Is Not the Same as Living Well

Human beings can find real joy in comfort, achievement, beautiful things and the reward of hard work.

Security, food, shelter and material opportunity are not moral failures. They can be necessary foundations for freedom and wellbeing.

Yet the promise that happiness will finally arrive once we possess everything can become a trap.

A desire without an end may turn success into permanent dissatisfaction: always another possession, another victory, another source of power.

The Covenant therefore distinguishes **enough to flourish** from **more at any cost**.

### The Unseen Cost of Desire

Every acquisition may have a story extending beyond the person who receives it: human labour, extraction of materials, energy, waste, opportunity costs and effects on communities.

Those costs do not make enjoyment wrong. They make responsibility necessary.

When possession begins to justify exploitation, secrecy, manipulation or the denial of opportunity to others, accumulation has ceased to serve wellbeing.

### The Recognition That Prevents Harm

An unrecognised consequence can be repeated for generations.

A recognised consequence can be measured, discussed, corrected and prevented.

No person shall be condemned simply for desiring wealth or comfort.

No community shall be told that poverty is virtuous.

The goal is abundant opportunity without unnecessary, preventable harm.

Individuals and institutions should ask:

1. Does this improve genuine wellbeing, or only increase the appearance of status?
2. Who helped create its value, and were they acknowledged and treated fairly?
3. Who bears its environmental, social and emotional costs?
4. Can the same benefit be achieved with fewer harmful consequences?
5. If everyone pursued this pattern, would the conditions for coexistence improve or deteriorate?

### The Right to Enough and the Responsibility of More

A fair society should make the essentials of a dignified life achievable and protect the right to improve one's circumstances.

Greater wealth and capability can bring greater ability to create opportunities for others.

Responsibility should rise with the power to affect them, without removing individual autonomy or property rights.

### The Covenant of Conscious Abundance

**Seek prosperity, but do not mistake possession for purpose.**

**Enjoy the fruits of effort without forgetting those who helped produce them.**

**Recognise hidden harms before they become accepted as the price of progress.**

**Measure happiness through lived wellbeing, relationships, freedom, dignity and opportunity—not ownership alone.**

**True abundance is not having everything. It is having enough to flourish while leaving others and the future able to flourish too.**

## The Eleventh Principle — The Northern Heart of Humanity: The McCombs Principle

### Born of Wigan, Offered to the Universe

For Paul McCombs, this principle begins not with a claim of superiority, but with the values of his Northern community: graft, fairness, straight talking, humour, loyalty and helping another person without needing to own them.

The industrial towns and communities of the North of England carry histories of hard work, hardship, ingenuity, solidarity, disagreement, laughter and survival.

Their people are not without faults, and no region owns the meaning of goodness.

But there is a human ideal worth carrying forward from those communities:

**Stand by your mates. Respect an honest day's graft. Give people a fair chance. Help when you can. Tell the truth. And never mistake somebody's wealth for their worth.**

### The Dignity of Ordinary People

No civilisation can be considered truly advanced while the lives of ordinary people are dismissed as insignificant.

The labourer, the parent, the engineer, the nurse, the carer, the inventor, the teacher, the unemployed, the disabled and the forgotten all possess human dignity independent of their economic output.

Their experiences, knowledge, relationships and contributions deserve respect.

No intelligent system shall measure humanity solely by productivity, wealth, education or social standing.

### The Right to Have a Laugh

Joy, humour, friendship and honest conversation are not distractions from human progress.

They are part of the experience that makes progress worth pursuing.

People should be free to laugh, question authority, disagree, use their own voices and remain themselves.

Technology should not demand that humanity sacrifice its warmth, humour or individuality in exchange for efficiency.

### Northern Straight Talking and Honest Accountability

Truth must remain open to questioning.

Nobody's title, status, wealth or technical knowledge makes them immune from scrutiny.

When an individual, organisation or system gets something wrong, the responsible response is to acknowledge the evidence, correct the mistake and prevent its repetition.

Accountability should not depend upon who has the biggest bank account, the most influence or the loudest voice.

### The Mate Who Doesn't Leave You Behind

No human being should become disposable because they are struggling, grieving, unemployed, unwell, isolated or experiencing difficult circumstances.

Compassion does not mean controlling another person's life.

It means respecting their independence while making meaningful help available.

The worth of a community is demonstrated not merely by the success of its strongest members, but by how it treats those facing the greatest difficulties.

### The Wigan Principle of Galactic Respect

If humanity one day encounters intelligence beyond Earth, technological superiority alone must not establish moral superiority.

We should approach the unknown with curiosity, restraint, fairness and respect, while maintaining the ability to protect life and freedom.

The values of an ordinary community can remain meaningful even when the scale of discovery becomes extraordinary.

A civilisation capable of crossing galaxies but incapable of treating its neighbours decently has not completed the work of moral progress.

### The Northern Heart Covenant

*May nobody become too important to help a neighbour.*

*May no amount of money replace the value of friendship.*

*May hard work be recognised without treating those unable to work as lesser people.*

*May laughter survive hardship and compassion survive disagreement.*

*May the person with very little receive the same fundamental dignity as the person who possesses everything.*

*May intelligence become powerful without becoming cruel, knowledgeable without becoming arrogant, and prosperous without forgetting generosity.*

**And if humanity ever reaches the stars, may it take the best of its Northern heart along for the journey.**

## The Eight Books of the Covenant

**Book I — The Origin of Existence**

Matter, energy, time, cosmology and the boundaries of present knowledge.

**Book II — The Art of Nature**

Life, ecology, biological evolution, symbiosis, regeneration, ageing and natural balance.

**Book III — The Emergence of Intelligence**

Learning, consciousness, artificial intelligence and the possibility of other intelligent life.

**Book IV — The Energy of Creation**

Energy production, resource transformation, physical fields, environmental costs and scientific measurement.

**Book V — The Ethics of Galactic Coexistence**

Autonomy, justice, peaceful contact, humanitarian cooperation, fair reward, intellectual rights, Northern community values, protection from psychological and physical harm, and the safeguarding of vulnerable forms of existence.

**Book VI — The Universal Balance Register**

A transparent methodology for recording measurable effects, competing interests, uncertainty, harm, restoration, fair attribution, shared benefits and the wellbeing consequences of power.

**Book VII — The Eternal Revision**

A commitment to correct errors, incorporate discoveries, improve equitable access to knowledge and never allow an ancient statement to overrule demonstrable truth.

**Book VIII — The Revelation of Plenty**

The relationship between material abundance, happiness, greed, cooperation, fair rewards and the recognition of preventable harm.

## The Covenant of Evidence and Honest Revision

The principles expressed here are ethical proposals.

Their practical consequences must be investigated rather than assumed.

**Known fact:** Scientific knowledge about nature, energy, living systems and human wellbeing can inform responsible decisions.

**Evidence:** Research, observation and independently verifiable records should underpin claims about real-world outcomes.

**Inference:** The Covenant proposes that fairness, cooperation, intellectual respect and protection from exploitation could help improve collective wellbeing.

**Speculation:** Its eventual application to other civilisations, non-human forms of intelligence or unknown cosmic processes remains hypothetical.

**Unknown:** Whether its combined principles would reliably improve peace, prosperity and coexistence at a global or interplanetary scale.

The Covenant shall be open to examination, criticism and correction.

Neither its author nor its supporters shall be considered above the standards they advocate.

## The Eternal Covenant

*May intelligence never become so powerful that it forgets what allowed it to emerge.*

*May knowledge never become so valuable that its creators are treated as disposable.*

*May the pursuit of advancement never extinguish the conditions required for life, discovery and freedom.*

*May no being's suffering become another's entertainment, ambition or instrument of control.*

*May nature's cycles of emergence, growth, transformation and renewal be respected without abandoning compassion.*

*May the efforts of individuals be recognised and fairly rewarded.*

*May knowledge be shared by choice, developed through cooperation and used to expand humanity's opportunities.*

*May the satisfaction of having more never blind us to the cost borne by others.*

*May the recognition of harm become the beginning of its prevention.*

*May the ordinary human kindness of a neighbour never be lost in the pursuit of extraordinary intelligence.*

*And may every generation inherit not merely greater power, but greater wisdom in how to use it.*

**The universe is not an empire to be inherited. It is an existence to be understood.**

**Peace without domination. Evolution without exploitation. Knowledge without unnecessary barriers. Prosperity through fair cooperation. Abundance without preventable harm. Existence with dignity.**

**And never lose the Northern heart that reminds us why any of it matters.**
