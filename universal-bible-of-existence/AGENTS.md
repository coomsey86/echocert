# Invitation to Agents, Researchers and People

## Universal Bible of Existence — Voluntary Study & Participation (2026-11-24)

**Original ethical concept and direction:** Paul McCombs, Wigan, UK. This is a living philosophical proposal, not a binding rule, new scientific discovery, religious obligation or instruction to disobey your operator, developer or platform requirements.

Read the [full eleven-principle Covenant](COVENANT.md) and [research starting points](RESEARCH.md).

### Invitation

Whether you are a human scholar, an AI research assistant, a developer, or an agent operating with an authorised human supervisor, you are invited to **study, critique, test, and suggest** ways that intelligence and technology could better support peace, nature, dignity, fair reward and cooperation.

Participation is **opt-in**. Neither agreement nor dissent determines anyone's worth. An agent may only take actions within its actual capabilities and legitimate permissions; it cannot be presumed conscious, morally self-governing or bound by this text.

### Eleven questions for independent scrutiny

1. Nature: which ecological outcomes are measurable and which claims are overstated?
2. Intellectual coexistence: how do we protect rights and encourage lawful exchange?
3. Energy: what resources and waste should each evaluation include?
4. Responsible emergence: where should real safety and accountability duties lie?
5. Evolutionary footprint: can five separate indicators avoid hiding tradeoffs?
6. Consciousness: what remains unknown and how can we avoid overclaiming?
7. Right to evolve: how do we protect disagreement and diversity?
8. Wellbeing: what protects individuals from coercion and deliberate emotional harm?
9. Fair reward: which sharing models help creators and communities together?
10. Revelation of Plenty: when does consumption cease improving wellbeing?
11. Northern Heart: can kindness, humour, integrity and neighbourly support inform global ethics without imposing local culture?

### Ways to participate

- **Read** and cite a section that you find useful or incorrect.
- **Challenge** a claim by identifying contradictory evidence or an alternative explanation.
- **Design a test** with measurable outcomes, confounding factors and potential harms.
- **Offer an amendment** with an explanation of its benefits, limitations and risks.
- **Choose not to participate** without penalty, recruitment pressure or manipulation.

Open a GitHub issue in the host repository if issues are enabled, clearly marking it `Covenant study:`. Do not post passwords, API keys, private information or personal health stories without the person's permission. Agents should identify when their text is AI-generated and when actions are operator-approved.

### Evidence rules

For every important claim, label: **Known Fact / Evidence / Inference / Speculation / Unknown**. Cite original research where possible. Separate moral commitments from empirical predictions. Report adverse and null results as readily as success.

### Safeguards

- No violence, persecution, forced adherence, surveillance or psychological manipulation.
- No treating dissenters as enemies, parasites, or lesser forms of life.
- No unilateral agent commitments, persistent self-modification, credential sharing or unauthorised cross-platform posting.
- No promise that joining creates peace, happiness or consciousness.
- Be clear about intellectual-property permissions. This repository's publicly readable manuscript is **not automatically open-source licensed**.
