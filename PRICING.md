# EchoCert Pricing

## Current offer

### EchoCert AI Evidence Audit — Founding Pilot

**£495 GBP, one-time and paid upfront after written scope approval.**

The price covers one defined AI evidence question or incident, using up to **100 approved non-sensitive files or 2 GB**, whichever comes first.

Included outputs:

- Source inventory and duplicate identification
- SHA-256 manifest and integrity receipts
- Verification results and exceptions
- Findings split into observed facts, supported inference and unresolved or unproven claims
- Human-readable report
- Portable Evidence Pack and verification instructions
- One factual or clerical correction round requested within five business days of delivery

Target delivery is within **seven business days** after payment clears, intake is complete and the evidence set is accepted.

[View the official EchoCert service website](https://echocert-evidence.mccombsp86.chatgpt.site)

## Scope and payment rules

- Exact scope is agreed in writing before payment.
- Payment must clear before intake and substantive work begin.
- The fee does not include production access, bespoke integrations, mobile-app delivery, software licences, legal advice or expert testimony.
- If EchoCert declines the case before substantive work begins, the fee is refunded in full.
- After substantive work begins, any refund is limited to the unperformed portion agreed in writing.
