# EchoCert — Indicative Founding Pricing

EchoCert is in controlled-pilot development. Pricing below is **indicative early-market pricing**, not a permanent tariff, and may change as pilot evidence establishes what customers value and support costs become clearer.

The public GitHub demonstration remains governed by the current BSL 1.1 licence. See [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) and [`LICENSING.md`](LICENSING.md).

---

## Controlled Pilot

**Selected participants: £0 for 2–4 weeks**

Purpose:

- Test a real evidence workflow
- Identify usability and reliability problems
- Measure whether EchoCert saves time or improves the evidence process
- Obtain structured feedback
- Establish whether the participant would pay for continued use

Pilot places should remain limited so support and feedback can be handled properly.

A free pilot is not intended to become indefinite free production use.

---

## EchoCert Professional — founding target

**Indicative target: £299/year per professional user**

Intended for solo professionals and small operators who need local-first evidence integrity and the desktop/mobile workflow.

Expected value proposition:

- EchoCert Elite desktop access under the applicable commercial licence
- Mobile companion access when released for the relevant build
- SHA-256 integrity receipts and verification
- Professional evidence reports / packs supported by the licensed build
- Product updates during the licence period
- Standard support appropriate to an early commercial product

Final packaging depends on pilot validation and release readiness.

---

## EchoCert Team — founding target

**Indicative target: from £999/year for a small team**

Intended for small inspection, compliance, field-service, legal-support or specialist teams.

Potential package:

- Multiple authorised users
- Desktop/mobile workflow
- Shared organisational templates where supported
- Priority onboarding
- Team-focused support

Exact seats, support commitments and features should be agreed before sale rather than promised before they exist.

---

## Business / specialist deployment

**Quoted after discovery or pilot**

For organisations requiring larger deployments, specialised onboarding, integration, custom workflow support or contractual service commitments.

Pricing should reflect actual scope, support burden and commercial value rather than an arbitrary enterprise number.

---

## Evidence / verification services

EchoCert may also support paid services where a customer wants the outcome without operating the software themselves.

Possible services include:

- Evidence-pack preparation
- Integrity verification and reporting
- Workflow setup
- Staff onboarding
- Documentation support

These services must remain within appropriate legal/professional boundaries and should not be represented as legal or forensic expert opinion unless the relevant qualification and engagement genuinely exist.

---

## Pricing principle

EchoCert should not compete by being the cheapest app in the market.

The commercial test is whether customers will pay for:

**simple field capture + preserved originals + verifiable SHA-256 integrity + professional desktop reporting.**

Prices should rise only when product reliability, support, validation and customer value justify it.
