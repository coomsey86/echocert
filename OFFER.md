# EchoCert AI Evidence Audit — Founding Pilot

EchoCert's current commercial offer is one tightly bounded managed service. It turns an agreed set of AI workflow records into an evidence pack another person can inspect and verify.

**Price: £495 GBP, one-time and paid upfront after written scope approval.**

[View the official EchoCert service website](https://echocert-evidence.mccombsp86.chatgpt.site)

---

## Best fit

The founding pilot is intended for UK business customers with one completed, representative non-production AI workflow, handover, dispute or incident that can be examined from approved records.

Good-fit examples include:

- An AI consultancy handing a workflow to a client
- An automation agency needing a neutral record of what was supplied and tested
- A professional team investigating a change, mismatch or disputed output
- A small organisation that needs a portable integrity record without granting production access

EchoCert confirms exact scope in writing before payment. It may decline unlawful, unsafe, prohibited or unsuitable work.

---

## Included scope

- One defined AI evidence question or incident
- Up to **100 approved non-sensitive files or 2 GB**, whichever comes first
- Source inventory and duplicate identification
- SHA-256 manifest and deterministic integrity receipts
- Verification results and recorded exceptions
- Findings separated into observed facts, supported inference and unresolved or unproven claims
- Human-readable report
- Portable Evidence Pack with verification instructions
- One factual or clerical correction round requested within five business days of delivery

Target delivery is within **seven business days** after all three gates are complete: payment has cleared, intake is complete and EchoCert has accepted the evidence set.

---

## Not included

- Production-system access
- Personal, highly sensitive, unlawful or prohibited material
- Live surveillance or provider-backend access
- Bespoke integration or custom feature development
- Legal advice, expert testimony or a guarantee of admissibility
- A judgment that an AI output is true, correct, safe or compliant
- Proof of authorship, complete capture or independently trusted time unless supported by separate evidence
- Mobile-app delivery or a software licence

## Payment and refund boundary

The £495 GBP fee is paid upfront only after the written scope is approved.

- If EchoCert declines the case before substantive work begins, the fee is refunded in full.
- After substantive work begins, any refund is limited to the unperformed portion agreed in writing.

Payment does not widen the agreed scope or remove the evidence and safety boundaries.

---

## What the result means

A matching SHA-256 digest supports the conclusion that the checked bytes match the bytes represented by the recorded digest. EchoCert reports what the retained evidence demonstrates and clearly labels inference and unresolved claims.

EchoCert does not call a simulation a customer result, does not treat duplicate files as independent corroboration and does not claim success before payment and verified delivery.
