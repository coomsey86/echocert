# EchoCert Commercial Offer

EchoCert is being developed as a local-first digital evidence integrity system with a Windows professional desktop product and an Android field-capture companion.

The early commercial offer is deliberately simple: prove value in a controlled pilot, then convert useful workflows into paid licences or services.

---

## Controlled Pilot — selected users

**2–4 weeks / no licence fee for selected pilot participants**

Best suited to:

- Inspection and field-service professionals
- Construction / QA / condition-record workflows
- Legal support and dispute preparation
- Compliance and internal audit
- Investigators and specialist consultants

Typical pilot flow:

1. Capture or select an important file.
2. Preserve the original where possible.
3. Create a SHA-256 integrity receipt.
4. Re-verify the file later.
5. Review/report the evidence in EchoCert Elite where appropriate.

The pilot is intended to test usability, reliability and willingness to pay — not to provide indefinite free production use.

## Evidence Integrity Service

For customers who need an integrity record or evidence pack but do not want to operate the software themselves, EchoCert may offer a managed service.

Possible outputs:

- SHA-256 integrity receipt
- Verification result
- Human-readable report
- Organised evidence pack
- Plain-English technical notes about what the integrity check does and does not prove

This service must not be represented as legal advice, forensic expert evidence or an admissibility guarantee unless the appropriate qualified professional engagement genuinely exists.

---

## Commercial principle

EchoCert should earn revenue by solving a real evidence-handling problem reliably.

**Capture or select → preserve → hash → receipt → verify → report.**

If pilots do not produce real continued-use demand, the correct response is to improve or narrow the product rather than inflate the sales claim.
