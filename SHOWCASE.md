## A confident answer is not an audit trail

An assistant says it sent an email. An agency says a workflow completed. A file arrives labelled “final”.

What can the retained records actually support?

EchoCert is being built to make that question easier to answer: preserve the relevant records, check their integrity, and separate supported findings from claims that still need evidence.

**Prepared is not sent. Accepted is not delivered. Delivered is not read.**

That distinction matters when a team has to explain its work to a client, investigate a disputed handover or check what an automated workflow recorded.

## Built for useful scrutiny

EchoCert’s approach is to show what was checked and what the evidence cannot establish. A file matching its recorded hash does not prove that its contents are true. Local timestamps are not independently trusted time.
