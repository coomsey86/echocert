# EchoCert FAQ

## What is EchoCert?

EchoCert is a local-first AI evidence and audit receipt tool. It records prompts, outputs, timestamps and metadata, then seals the record with an integrity hash.

---

## What problem does it solve?

AI-generated content is easy to copy, edit, lose or dispute. EchoCert helps create a record of what existed, when it existed and whether it changed later.

---

## Does EchoCert prove an AI output is true?

No. EchoCert does not prove truth. It proves integrity around the recorded evidence.

---

## Does EchoCert prove legal ownership?

No. EchoCert can support evidence records, but it does not decide legal authorship or ownership by itself.

---

## Does EchoCert inspect model internals?

No. EchoCert works with prompts, outputs and files. It does not inspect private model internals.

---

## Is this the full commercial product?

No. This public repository is a demonstration build. Private commercial workflows, production logic, signing infrastructure, client systems and protected implementation details are not included.

---

## Can I use EchoCert commercially?

Commercial or production use requires a separate commercial licence. See [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) and [`LICENSING.md`](LICENSING.md).

---

## What should not be uploaded to this repository?

Do not upload client data, real legal evidence, personal records, passwords, tokens, private keys, health information, financial information or private commercial workflows.

---

## Who is EchoCert for?

EchoCert is aimed at compliance teams, AI governance teams, legal support, internal audit, researchers, consultants, agencies and businesses adopting AI tools.
