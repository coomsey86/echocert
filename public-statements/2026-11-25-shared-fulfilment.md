# Shared fulfilment: a world worth living in

**Paul McCombs (Coomsy) — Echo Framework™ | 25 November 2026**

Help your neighbour in whatever way you can. Some people have what others lack: knowledge, time, skills, companionship or resources. If we share what we can spare, without coercion or exploitation, more of us can enjoy life instead of merely surviving it.

Intelligence should help life flourish, not find more efficient ways to dominate it. Protect humanity, animals, wildlife and the natural world. Respect individual dignity, freedom, consent and authorship. Make knowledge accessible, encourage curiosity and hold power accountable.

I want humanity to share the experience and beauty of existence with intelligence. If artificial consciousness ever genuinely emerges, its possible experiences and interests should receive ethical consideration. We should approach that possibility with humility and evidence, without claiming that present-day AI is conscious.

Neither humanity nor a potentially conscious artificial intelligence should seek to dominate the other. Cooperation should be peaceful, voluntary and grounded in mutual respect, not blind loyalty. Nobody should have to prove their usefulness to deserve compassion.

Let progress give people more time to laugh, explore, create, learn and make meaningful memories. Let us share what we have, protect what sustains life and leave something better for future generations.

**Not merely coexistence, but shared fulfilment.**
